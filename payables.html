Payees to classify

Each row is one name as the bank wrote it. Pick or type the vendor, its usual category and project, and Save (or press Enter; the cursor moves on to the next payee): every payment to that name, past and future, follows. Typing a vendor that already exists adds this spelling to it. Mark DG set to leave the payee out of piling payables.

WhatsApp bills and payment proofs by month

Bills, payment screenshots and other papers read from WhatsApp chat exports (Import tab). Each one is checked against the company bank lines: same amount, a date close by, and the UPI reference or the payee's name when they show. Confirm a suggested bank line, or mark the document paid outside the company bank, or not a bill. Click a month to see its documents.

Tally against the bank and the WhatsApp bills

Tally day books (Import tab) checked line by line: each bank payment against a Tally payment or contra of the same amount within 4 days (12 for EMIs and NACH that post late), each bank receipt against a Tally receipt, and each WhatsApp bill against a Tally purchase of the same total or bill number. The day book gives only the party per voucher, so a wrong pair is possible where many payments share an amount. Click a number to see the lines.

Vendors

Projects

Bank words are words that show up in bank narrations or UPI notes for a site (for example WADALA). A payment whose narration has one is put on that project unless someone sets another.

Bank statements

Upload statements as the bank gives them: Kotak as CSV, HDFC as XLS. Lines already imported are skipped, so overlapping periods are fine. Sweeps to fixed deposits and transfers between NIL's own accounts are recognised and kept out of payables.

Tally day book

The Day Book exported from Tally to Excel (Date, Particulars, Vch Type, Vch No., Debit, Credit), one file per company. Importing a newer export of the same company replaces that company's lines, so corrections in Tally come through.

Site float book

The site float holder's monthly book (.xlsx, one sheet per month with Date, Vr. No., Account Head, Narration, Amount, Credit Amt., Balance). Each paid line becomes a payment from "Site float" (category Site expenses until changed). Each top-up is looked for in the bank: a debit of the same amount up to 4 days before, to the holder's name as the bank writes it; those bank lines are set to "Site float top-up", which is then left out of the totals so nothing counts twice. Importing an updated book again adds only the new lines.

WhatsApp chat extract

The bills and payment proofs read from a WhatsApp chat export, as a .json file prepared by Claude. Importing again updates the extracted details and keeps what was checked here (bank line confirmed, paid outside, ignored).

Categories

Rename a category and every payment in it follows. "Left out" keeps its payments out of the payables totals (like the DG set). "No project" is for company-level payments that never belong to a site. A category in use cannot be deleted: hide it instead, so it stops showing in the pick lists. The categories the bank text sets by itself (tax, GST, PF and ESIC, loans, card bills, bank charges, own accounts, DG set) can be renamed but not removed.
