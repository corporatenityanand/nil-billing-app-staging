Reading the numbers

Invoiced due = tax invoices raised, less TDS, retention, advance recovery and other debits, less amounts received.

Proforma = proformas not yet converted to tax invoices. They are not yet legally due, but they are work billed.

Credits = advances or receipts above what the bill nets to. They reduce what you can chase.

Retention held is locked with clients until release, so it is not in the receivable figures.

Adds up the work not yet billed for one client and site at the BOQ rates. The bill form is filled in for you (proforma or tax invoice, your choice); adding the bill marks these entries billed so they are never billed twice. Deleting that bill frees them again.
